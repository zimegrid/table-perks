Step 1

Add Branch

Create the merchant branch first. This is required before staff can be assigned.

Button: Top right → Add Branch
1

From the left sidebar, open Branch Management → Branches.

2

On the Branches page, click the Add Branch button in the top right corner.

3

Complete the branch form including branch name, address, optional order URL, and map location.

4

Set Active to ON. Use Main Branch only if this is the primary location.

5

Click Add Branch to save.

Example Data

Branch NameRozana Restaurant - Lilburn
Address4800 Lawrenceville Hwy A1, Lilburn, GA 30047
Order URLhttps://rozanarestaurantdemo.com/order
ActiveEnabled
Main BranchEnabled (if primary)

Why this step matters

Staff and role assignments depend on at least one branch being available. Always create the primary branch before staff setup.

Step 2

Create Roles

Roles must be created before staff members can be added. This controls access inside the merchant panel.

Button: Create Role
1

Open Branch Management → Permissions from the left sidebar.

2

Click Create Role.

3

Enter the role name, then tick the required permissions.

4

Click Create Role to save.

Recommended Main Role

Branch Manager
  • Branches
  • Staffs
  • Customers
  • Stamps
  • Issue Stamp
  • Menu Items
  • Categories
  • Banners

Operational Role

Waiter / Cashier
  • Customers
  • Stamps
  • Issue Stamp
  • Optional: Menu Items

Marketing Role

Marketing Staff
  • Customers
  • Banners
  • Optional: Stamps

Step 3

Step 5

Add Menu Items

Menu items help define the products associated with the merchant and can support item-based stamp programs.

Button: Top right → Add Menu
1

Open Menu Management → Menu Items.

2

Click Add Menu in the top right corner.

3

Enter item name, description, image, price, and category.

4

If needed, add Variants for item options such as size or flavor.

5

Set the item to Active and save.

Example Menu Items

ItemHummus Plate
CategoryAppetizers
Price$8.99
Variant ExampleLatte → Small / Medium / Large

Best practice

For Greggs-style item loyalty, create item-specific menu entries such as Coffee, Breakfast Roll, and Dessert so the stamp logic can match merchant products more clearly.

Step 6

Create Stamp Cards

Create item-specific stamp programs such as coffee, breakfast roll, or dessert loyalty cards.

Button: Top right → Add Stamp
1

Open Stamps from the left sidebar.

2

Click Add Stamp in the top right corner.

3

Enter the stamp name, reward, description, stamp image, icon style, and required stamp count.

4

Set Active to ON. Enable Renewable if the card should restart after redemption.

5

Click Create Stamp.

Recommended Greggs-style Stamp Cards

Stamp 1Coffee Stamp Card
RewardFree Coffee
Stamps Required10
Stamp 2Breakfast Roll Stamp Card
RewardFree Breakfast Roll
Stamp 3Dessert Stamp Card
RewardFree Dessert

Recommended setup

Use item-specific stamp cards instead of one generic loyalty card when the merchant wants separate programs for coffee, breakfast rolls, or other product lines.

Step 9

Create Campaigns

Use saved templates to send campaigns to all enrolled customers in the merchant’s loyalty audience.

Email Campaigns → Campaigns
1

Open Email Campaigns → Campaigns.

2

Click Create Campaign in the top right corner.

3

Enter the subject line, preview text, from name, reply-to email, and optional internal campaign title.

4

Select a saved template from Choose saved templates.

5

Review the preview screen, then click Start Campaign to send to all customers.

Example Campaign

Subject LineWelcome to Rozana Rewards - Your Free Stamp Is Ready
Reply-Tosupport@tableperks.com
AudienceAll enrolled customers
ImportantStarting a campaign sends the email to all customers enrolled for this merchant. Always review the preview screen before sending.

Step 10

Scan Customer QR, Add Stamp, or Redeem

Use the QR scanner to identify an enrolled customer, then add stamps or redeem rewards based on progress.

Sidebar → Scan QR
1

Open Scan QR from the left sidebar.

2

Scan the enrolled customer’s QR code or manually enter the customer code if needed.

3

Review the customer details after the scan is successful.

4

Select the relevant stamp card (for example: Coffee Stamp Card or Breakfast Roll Stamp Card).

5

Click Add Stamp to award progress or Redeem when the customer qualifies for the reward.

Example Usage

Customer CodeCUST002
Stamp CardCoffee Stamp Card
ActionAdd Stamp or Redeem
