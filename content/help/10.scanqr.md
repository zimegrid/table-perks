## Steps

1. Open **Scan QR** from the left sidebar.
2. Scan the enrolled customer's QR code or manually enter the customer code if needed.
3. Review the customer details after the scan is successful.
4. Select the relevant stamp card (for example: Coffee Stamp Card or Breakfast Roll Stamp Card).
5. Click **Add Stamp** to award progress or **Redeem** when the customer qualifies for the reward.

## Example Usage

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Customer Code | CUST002 |
| Stamp Card | Coffee Stamp Card |
| Action | Add Stamp or Redeem |
