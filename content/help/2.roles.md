## Steps

1. Open **Branch Management → Permissions** from the left sidebar.
2. Click **Create Role**.
3. Enter the role name, then tick the required permissions.
4. Click **Create Role** to save.

## Recommended Roles

**Branch Manager** (recommended main role)
- Branches
- Staffs
- Customers
- Stamps
- Issue Stamp
- Menu Items
- Categories
- Banners

**Waiter / Cashier** (operational role)
- Customers
- Stamps
- Issue Stamp
- Optional: Menu Items

**Marketing Staff** (marketing role)
- Customers
- Banners
- Optional: Stamps
