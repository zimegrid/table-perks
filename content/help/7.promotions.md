## Steps

1. Open **Promotions → Banners**.
2. Click **Add Banner** in the top right corner.
3. Enter the banner title, optional description, upload an image, and set optional link URL.
4. Choose priority, start date, end date, and set **Active Status**.
5. Click **Create Banner**.

## Example Banner

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | Join Rozana Rewards & Get a Free Welcome Stamp |
| Priority | 1 |
| Status | Active |
