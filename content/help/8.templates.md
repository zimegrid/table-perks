## Steps

1. Open **Email Campaigns → Email Templates**.
2. Click **Create Template**.
3. Enter the template name and build the HTML content in the editor.
4. If needed, click **Browse Templates** to choose from integrated Mailchimp-style templates.
5. Set **Active Status** to ON and save.

## Example Template

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Template Name | Rozana Rewards – Welcome Email |
| Use Case | Welcome new loyalty customers |
