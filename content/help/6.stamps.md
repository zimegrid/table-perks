## Steps

1. Open **Stamps** from the left sidebar.
2. Click **Add Stamp** in the top right corner.
3. Enter the stamp name, reward, description, stamp image, icon style, and required stamp count.
4. Set **Active** to ON. Enable **Renewable** if the card should restart after redemption.
5. Click **Create Stamp**.

## Recommended Greggs-style Stamp Cards

| | |
|---|---|
| Stamp 1 | Coffee Stamp Card |
| Reward | Free Coffee |
| Stamps Required | 10 |
| Stamp 2 | Breakfast Roll Stamp Card |
| Reward | Free Breakfast Roll |
| Stamp 3 | Dessert Stamp Card |
| Reward | Free Dessert |

## Recommended setup

Use item-specific stamp cards instead of one generic loyalty card when the merchant wants separate programs for coffee, breakfast rolls, or other product lines.
