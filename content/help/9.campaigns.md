## Steps

1. Open **Email Campaigns → Campaigns**.
2. Click **Create Campaign** in the top right corner.
3. Enter the subject line, preview text, from name, reply-to email, and optional internal campaign title.
4. Select a saved template from **Choose saved templates**.
5. Review the preview screen, then click **Start Campaign** to send to all customers.

## Example Campaign

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Subject Line | Welcome to Rozana Rewards - Your Free Stamp Is Ready |
| Reply-To | support@tableperks.com |
| Audience | All enrolled customers |

> **Important:** Starting a campaign sends the email to all customers enrolled for this merchant. Always review the preview screen before sending.
