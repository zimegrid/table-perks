## Steps

1. Open **Scan QR** from the left sidebar.
2. Scan the enrolled customer's QR code or manually enter their customer code.
3. On the **Issue Points** tab, enter the **Invoice Number**, **Purchase Amount**, and **Transaction Date**, then click **Issue Points**.
4. To redeem a reward instead, switch to the **Redeem Points** tab, select an eligible reward from the customer's list, and click **Redeem Selected**.
5. Confirm the **Invoice Number** and **Issue Date**, then click **Submit Redeem**.

## Example Data

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Customer Code | CUST002 |
| Purchase Amount | $42.50 |
| Invoice Number | INV-1042 |
