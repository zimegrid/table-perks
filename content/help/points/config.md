## Steps

1. Open **Points → Configuration** from the left sidebar.
2. On the **Point System** card, set your base rate: enter **Dollars per Point** for the first slab (e.g. $1 spent = 1 point).
3. To reward bigger purchases at a better rate, click **Add Slab**, then set a **Min Purchase ($)** and a lower **Dollars per Point**. Each new slab must have a higher minimum purchase and a faster earning rate than the slab before it.
4. Repeat to add more slabs if needed. Remove a slab with the trash icon (the first slab can't be removed).
5. Click **Save Slabs** to save your earning rules.

## Example Data

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Slab 1 | $1 spent = 1 point (no minimum) |
| Slab 2 | Min Purchase $50, $0.80 spent = 1 point |

## Why this step matters

These earning rules drive how many points a customer receives every time staff record a purchase from the Scan QR screen — set this up before enrolling customers.
